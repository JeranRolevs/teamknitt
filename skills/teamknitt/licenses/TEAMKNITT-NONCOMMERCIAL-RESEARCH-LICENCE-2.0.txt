3. Use and eligibility definitions

"Research Use" means use for academic or scientific investigation, research
training, research experimentation, reproduction of results or validation of
findings. It includes such work whether formal or informal, published or private,
and whether undertaken by an individual or an organization. Ordinary learning,
hobby activity or software testing that is not part of academic or scientific

Software. Actual purpose and conduct determine the category, not a task label,
software mode, affiliation or description chosen to avoid this licence.

"Commercial Use" includes use for commercial advantage, business operations,
commercial deployment or monetary compensation; internal or commissioned R&D
intended to support a commercial product, service or business; selling or
commercially licensing the Software; or offering its functionality as a commercial
hosted service. A use can be commercial even if access is free. Ordinary academic
salaries, public or independent charitable research grants, and donations meeting
section 6 do not by themselves make otherwise Permitted Academic Research
commercial. Ordinary pay for genuinely noncommercial, non-research work also
does not by itself make that work commercial; actual purpose and beneficiary
arrangements control.

"Qualifying Economy" means an economy classified as low-income or
lower-middle-income in the World Bank's official Country and Lending Groups
income classification in force at the time of use:
https://datahelpdesk.worldbank.org/knowledgebase/articles/906519-world-bank-country-and-lending-groups
Upper-middle-income, high-income and unclassified economies do not qualify.
The income group, not a regional, lending or other grouping, controls. If the
classification or an institution's location cannot be established, separate
written permission is required for Research Use.

"Eligible Academic Institution" means a public or nonprofit university, college
or comparable educational institution legally recognized by the competent
educational authority, whose primary mission is education and public-interest
academic research or research training. It must be legally established and
genuinely headquartered in a Qualifying Economy, with its principal educational
and academic-research operations there. Its departments and research centers
qualify only while genuinely part of, or owned and controlled by, that institution
and under its independent academic governance. A local registration, postal
address, campus, affiliate or intermediary does not make an otherwise ineligible
institution eligible. An organization or unit excluded by section 4 cannot qualify.

"Authorized Academic User" means a currently enrolled student, faculty or staff
member, or formally appointed researcher acting solely on behalf of an Eligible
Academic Institution for its Permitted Academic Research. An appointment,
enrollment or university account gives no permission for work on behalf of
another employer, client or commercial beneficiary.

"Commercial Entity" means a for-profit business, commercial research organization,
company, or an entity acting on behalf of one in connection with the Software or
project. "Control" means the direct or indirect ability, alone or jointly with
others, to direct or decisively influence governance, management, budgets,
staffing, research priorities, publication or intellectual-property arrangements.
Control can arise from ownership, voting or appointment rights, contracts,
funding dependency or other arrangements, including through an intermediary.
A donation meeting section 6 does not by itself constitute Control.

"Permitted Academic Research" means noncommercial Research Use conducted by an
Eligible Academic Institution under its own independent academic direction and
for its educational or public-interest research mission. The institution must
retain control of the research agenda, conduct and results and freedom to
publish, subject to applicable law, research ethics and privacy obligations.
The work must not be performed on behalf of, commissioned by, or operated
primarily as research capacity for a Commercial Entity. Sections 4 and 6 apply
to its governance, funding, sponsor and beneficiary arrangements.

    Commercial Entity, acting on its behalf, or operating primarily as its R&D

(c) Commercial Use, commissioned company research, or research providing dedicated
    services, proprietary deliverables, development work or other privileged
    commercial benefits to a Commercial Entity;
(d) use through an academic account, appointment, student, contractor, affiliate,
    intermediary or shared infrastructure to obtain for an ineligible entity
    rights it could not obtain directly; or
(e) Research Use by or for an institution outside the geographic requirements,
    including through a campus, affiliate, researcher or intermediary in a
    Qualifying Economy.

Actual governance, funding, work and beneficiary arrangements control. Academic
names, nonprofit registration, educational email addresses, affiliations, public
papers or acknowledgement of the Software do not establish research eligibility.
Benefits from findings available to the public on equal terms are not by
themselves privileged commercial benefits or a grant of research eligibility.
Eligible institutions must not lend or extend their permission to others.
These research exclusions do not replace the separate test for genuinely
noncommercial, non-research use under section 2(a).
