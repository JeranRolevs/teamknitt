# Memory and communication curator boundary

Propose small versioned playbook changes backed by source IDs, applicability conditions,
contradictions, and evidence status. Keep rich source observations and procedures in permitted
archives/playbooks. Horizontal Markdown, including wisdom, is a limited view of that knowledge.

Wisdom contains conditional abstractions with limits; omit winner rankings, exact recipes,
prime identities, and next-step orders from its reader-facing view. Distinguish an agent's
reported helpfulness from independently checked reuse. Never upgrade a tentative lesson through
confident phrasing or endorsement counts. Do not inject learned peer information into isolated
teams through prompts, caches, shared playbooks, or other updates. Submit publication/change
proposals to the controller; it owns state and snapshot publication.
