# Statistical analysis in teamknitt

Statistical methods are not theories of teams. Standard and Complexity are explanatory
orientations; neither selects an analysis, changes a model or raises a budget.

teamknitt supports descriptive process records and, when a qualified domain requires it,
explicit frequentist count/interval analysis. The memory-policy example reports independent-run
denominators and exact Clopper–Pearson intervals. A Beta quantile used to calculate that
frequentist interval is not a Bayesian prior or posterior analysis.

Bayesian/probabilistic analysis, prior elicitation and model-assisted prior selection are not
options in teamknitt. Unsupported legacy settings fail before provider initialization. Never
silently replace an old method or reinterpret its results. Historical metadata may retain its
original method label without offering it for new execution.

Record the question, estimand, comparator, independent observation unit, sample count,
uncertainty method, assumptions, missingness, multiplicity if relevant, and analysis timing.
A model's confidence is not a p-value, confidence interval or evidence of task correctness.
When analysis is unnecessary or unsupported, record that state and its reason rather than
fabricating results. Do not infer scientific or team-configuration superiority from a short
software fixture or one task execution.
