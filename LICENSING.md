## Three use categories — research is an exception, not a loophole

1. **Noncommercial, non-research use is free.** This includes genuinely noncommercial ordinary
   learning, hobbies, private entertainment and community activities that are not Research Use.
   No qualifying-country or academic-affiliation test applies to this category.
2. **Research is free only under the Labtune-based academic conditions below.** This rule
   overrides the general noncommercial permission. **Noncommercial research outside qualifying
   low-income/lower-middle-income academic institutions requires separate written permission.**
   Personal, nonprofit, charitable, public or governmental status, public funding, unpaid work,
   open publication or an academic account does not create a research exemption.
3. **Commercial use requires a separate written licence before use.** Contact Goran Mitevski
   for Pioneertown B.V.; a request or acknowledgement is not permission.

Research Use includes academic/scientific investigation, research training, research experiments,
reproduction of results and validation of findings, whether formal/informal, public/private,
individual or institutional. Ordinary learning or software testing is not automatically research;
actual purpose and conduct control. Calling research a hobby, task or brainstorming session does
not bypass the research conditions. A workflow's software mode does not decide legal eligibility.

Commercial use includes business operations, commercial deployment/advantage, internal or
commissioned commercial R&D, selling/licensing the software commercially and commercial hosted
services. Free access or public findings do not by themselves make commercial work noncommercial.
Ordinary academic salaries and otherwise permitted public/independent charitable grants do not
alone make qualifying research commercial. Ordinary pay for genuinely noncommercial, non-research
work also does not by itself make it commercial; actual purpose and beneficiaries control.
See LICENSE for the definitions and mixed activities; obtain separate permission if uncertain.

## Research eligibility — the Labtune basis is retained

Free research permission is limited to:

- **Eligible institutions:** legally recognized public or nonprofit universities, colleges or
  comparable educational institutions whose primary mission is education and public-interest
  academic research or research training, legally established and genuinely headquartered in a
  qualifying economy, with their principal educational/research operations there. Departments
  and centers must genuinely belong to, or be owned and controlled by, the institution under
  independent academic governance.
- **Eligible users:** current students, faculty, staff or formally appointed researchers acting
  solely for that institution's permitted research, not another employer, client or commercial
  beneficiary. Independent researchers without a qualifying affiliation require permission.
- **Qualifying economies:** World Bank **low-income or lower-middle-income** economies under
  the official [Country and Lending Groups](https://datahelpdesk.worldbank.org/knowledgebase/articles/906519-world-bank-country-and-lending-groups)
  income classification **in force at the time of use**. Upper-middle-income, high-income and
  unclassified economies do not qualify. Nationality, a computer/cloud location, an affiliate,
  local registration or a campus cannot substitute for genuine institutional eligibility.
- **Eligible projects:** independent, noncommercial academic research under the institution's
  own direction and public-interest/educational mission. It must retain control of agenda,
  conduct and results and freedom to publish, subject to law, ethics and privacy.

Company labs, commercial consultancies, startups, for-profit educational institutions, commercial
joint ventures and university-owned commercial spinouts do not receive free research permission.
Company-controlled nonprofits, foundations, university-hosted labs and other corporate research
proxies also fail eligibility. Commissioned company work, proprietary deliverables, dedicated
commercial research capacity and other privileged commercial benefits require separate permission.
Routing work through a student, affiliate or qualifying partner does not extend the permission.

### Research funding and donations

Company cash or in-kind support is allowed for free research only as a genuinely **unrestricted
donation** to an otherwise eligible institution/project. It must not buy:

- control of governance, research direction, staff or resource allocation;
- commissioned research, services, dedicated capacity or proprietary deliverables;
- exclusive, preferential or advance access to data, findings or software changes;
- ownership, licences, options, first refusal or other preferential research/IP rights; or
- publication vetoes, approval rights or commercial publication restrictions/delays.

Side agreements and pass-through foundations cannot avoid the conditions. Ordinary donor
acknowledgement without those benefits is allowed. Buying general-purpose computing, model APIs,
equipment or ancillary technical services is allowed for the institution's research but gives
suppliers no independent research permission. General benefits from publicly available findings
on equal terms are not by themselves privileged commercial benefits.

### Continuing eligibility

Research eligibility must hold throughout use and be supportable with reasonable evidence on
request: institution, current country classification, affiliation, governance, funding and rights
arrangements. If these cease to qualify, further research needs separate written permission.
The general noncommercial permission is not a fallback for ineligible research. Uncertain cases
require permission before research, not an assumed exemption.

## Modifications and sharing are conditionally allowed

For Permitted Uses, the licence grants use, execution, copying, study, modification and sharing
of original or modified copies **without a software licence fee**. This restores the Labtune
approach; it is not the blanket modification/redistribution exclusion of the earlier 1.0 terms.

Copies or substantial portions you share must retain LICENSE, LICENSING.md, copyright/author,
attribution and required third-party notices. Identify changes. Distributed changes to the
software must use the same terms; do not weaken noncommercial use, research eligibility,
geographic/institutional restrictions, independence, funding or anti-circumvention conditions.
Each recipient obtains rights directly only for its own Permitted Uses. Sharing, a public fork
or a university account does not transfer someone else's research eligibility or authorize
commercial use. Contributions and development commands remain subject to these conditions.

## Acceptance, termination and liability

All grants are conditional on compliance. Breach ends permissions automatically; activities
requiring them must stop, and reinstatement needs written permission. The pasted tone example's
patent grant, patent-defense clause, automatic 32-day cure and organization-wide funding exemption
are **not** carried forward. The original Labtune-style reserved-rights, automatic termination,
as-is/no-warranty and liability limitations apply. Fair use and other statutory rights that cannot
be restricted remain unaffected. No trademark rights or additional licences are implied.

## Earlier copies, results and other rights

Version 2.0 applies to software offered under it, starting with the **0.9.0 development version**.
It does not purport to revoke or rewrite grants already made for earlier copies, including
copies offered under Teamknitt Noncommercial Licence 1.0. Earlier release notes describe their
own terms; the new research exception is not a retroactive withdrawal of earlier grants. This
update does not change the separate Labtune upstream project's licence or existing workspaces.

The licensor can grant only rights it controls. Separately licensed dependencies/materials keep
their own terms and notices. Independent inputs, data, findings and outputs are not relicensed
merely through use; software material included in them remains subject to applicable permission.
Provider, service and data terms and fees remain separate. Qualified legal review is recommended
before publication, including how research/non-research boundaries apply to a particular activity.
