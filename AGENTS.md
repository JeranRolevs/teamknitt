# Standalone teamknitt

teamknitt is based on Labtune, with its own runtime under teamknitt.engine and one console
command. No upstream package import, CLI delegation or parent checkout is allowed. Preserve
PROVENANCE.md, Goran Mitevski's authorship, Pioneertown B.V. and the current legal texts.
Preserve the brief research follow-up credit to Goran Mitevski’s 2022/2023 thesis work on
distributed teams and creative processes. Do not reproduce the abstract, formal thesis
citation or method details in product explanations. Use direct, descriptive headings,
with the user’s product-definition sentence as the main title. The wordmark is yellow
text with a brown text outline, not a filled rectangle. Research lineage does not imply
validated AI-team measures or authorize automatic participation control. Keep full thesis
files, student identifiers and organizational datasets outside exports.

The user sets tasks, brainstorming, goals or research, and explicitly chooses topologies,
models, communication, tools, delegation and budgets. Keep all 14 routing presets and
1–16-member bounds. Views never optimize, change or evaluate the team automatically.

Group team views by Non-system (collaborative intelligence; shared models/member reports) and
System (distributed problem-solving; differentiated transactive memory; interactive team
cognition/coordination; dynamics). Preserve historical Standard/Complexity presentation aliases.
Explanatory locus is distinct from compositional/compilational emergence and processes/states/
outcomes. Follow docs/TEAM_RESEARCH.md (9 Nov 2026 · @Goran). IPO/IMOI includes feedback and
conditional relations; do not call all conventional models linear. Human-team theory and
intervention findings are a design basis, not validated AI-team effects. Trust, psychological
safety, cohesion, efficacy and potency remain unmeasured. Transactive memory is differentiated,
not shared overlap. Shared-plan agreement, reference conformity, perceived alignment,
understanding and task correctness remain distinct. Keep original scholarly titles.

Operator requests are drafts by default, explicit --apply to queue, run/configuration-bound,
private and acknowledged at a dependency-wave boundary. Current-wave work may finish. Pause
keeps the shared lock and original wall deadline; resume resets no counters; stop is terminal
and cannot confirm incomplete work. Feedback is bounded task context, not a permission grant.
Receipts, context assembly, understanding and effects are distinct. Never add a browser write/
execution API implicitly; planned disturbances require explicit backup/lock-protected apply.

Complexity science is broader than Gorman et al. or one tradition; Santa Fe is not an
endorsement or a validated team model. Never fabricate emergence, adaptation, causal,
psychological, attractor, synchrony or fractal findings. Optional saved teammate probes remain
separate-context evidence, not an official CIB score or capabilities attributed to this team.

Bayesian/probabilistic analysis and prior workflows are not available in teamknitt; reject
legacy selected methods, never silently convert them. Original upstream functionality is not
modified. Preserve explicit credentials/model bindings and budgets during migration. Drafts
and migrations require explicit application, private backups and the shared run lock.
Keep resources and canonical diagrams synchronized. Publication needs user authorization.


# Multiple native teams

Collections under teamknitt.teams share one task and bounded total request/time/concurrency
limits while retaining each team's explicit native models, roles, routes and local limits.
Inter-team communication defaults to none. Only the designated main member's validated prior-
wave output may enter configured directed links; no implicit whole-team/private-artifact export,
automatic winner, merge, model choice or budget multiplication. Connected components freeze
frames at explicit phase barriers; unrelated components remain independent.

External style URLs are references only, not permission to copy/adapt/import foreign prompts,
skills, templates, configurations, code or CLIs. Only explicitly local user-authored style text
may become scoped context; a label or reference does not implement its source protocol. Keep
metadata/content and queued/applied/understood/outcome distinctions. Collection controls remain
native, run-bound and deadline-preserving. No browser write/execution/import endpoints. Preserve
single-team history and use docs/MULTI_TEAM.md and the canonical diagram specifications.
