NO AGGREGATE COGNITION SCORE
Observe the distinctions.
Role labels do not certify expertise. Shared plans can be wrong. An “in sync” self-report can coexist with conflicting plans. Domain outcomes require independent evaluation.


AN ADDITIONAL TOOL, NOT THE CONFIGURATOR
Observe the team as a dynamical system
Selected concepts from Gorman et al. (2017). Descriptive software traces do not validate human-team constructs.
Local variation, global continuity, disturbance response and informational coupling can be inspected alongside members, artifacts and coordination. The tool does not rank your team or choose a better topology.
Attractor, synchronization and fractal dimensions remain explicitly not estimated: short, uneven workflow waves do not justify those inferences.
Enable/disable or select dimensions in observations in team.yaml. No paid judge, model call or automatic organizational change.


THE CONFIGURATION BACKBONE
Foundations and Theoretical Perspectives
of Distributed Team Cognition
Edited by Michael D. McNeese, Eduardo Salas and Mica R. Endsley · CRC Press · first edition, 2021. The volume brings multiple perspectives to situated cognition across people, artifacts, technology and work. This software operationalizes selected distinctions; it does not claim to reproduce the book’s human-team studies.
Source consulted: user-supplied 246-page volume. No book text, images or simulation code are distributed with the application.


BUILDING ON LABTUNE
The original configuration idea
Goran Mitevski’s Labtune makes the organization of probabilistic discovery a researcher-controlled choice. teamknitt uses Labtune as its execution engine and extends the configuration idea to user-set tasks, ideas and goals, with distributed cognition and monitoring.
TEMPORAL EXTENSION
Teams as dynamical systems
Gorman, Dunbar, Grimm & Gipson (2017) inform the interaction-level perspective and controlled disturbances. Short workflows do not establish attractors, long memory, power laws or causal coupling.
