EXPLICIT OPERATOR AUTHORITY
Intervention desk
Choose a change, review its scope, then authorize it in the CLI from the selected workspace directory. This browser cannot queue requests or execute changes.
A queued request is not an applied intervention. Context assembly is not understanding. An applied intervention is not evidence that it improved the team. No automatic organizational improvement, model change or budget increase.




BETWEEN RUNS / INPUTS & CONDITIONS
Configuration changes
Staffing, role definitions, task framing, sources, procedures and routes are explicit choices.
Review the brief and success criteria in the local task file. The simple builder regenerates bindings only after --apply --reset-bindings, with a private backup; custom bindings remain a manual edit.
Active or paused runs retain their configuration and shared workspace lock. Model, tool, statistical and budget settings are never changed by an intervention request.


BEFORE A RUN / PLANNED DISTURBANCE
Scheduled disturbances
Schedule an actor, representation or directed-link gap, with a wave and duration you choose.
This example is a draft. Add --apply only after review. It changes the next run's plan—not an active run—and preserves roles, task text and engine settings.
A software condition is not human cross-training, model-weight training, evidence of adaptation or a causal intervention study.


DURING A RUN / SAFE BOUNDARY
Feedback, pause, resume or stop
Controls act before the next dependency wave; already-approved current-wave work may finish.
No command is executed here. Preview first; add --apply in the CLI to queue your reviewed request.
Feedback is private, targeted future main-member context, bounded by the existing representation word cap. It grants no tool or source permissions. Pause retains the original wall-time deadline and request counters. Stop is terminal; no automatic restart or recovery.


AFTER / HUMAN REVIEW
Outcome review
An input change, a process change and an outcome improvement are different claims.
Inspect partial or complete contributions locally. Check the brief, correctness, source use, alternatives and unresolved items; do not substitute agreement or a completed workflow for goal achievement.
Before claiming an effect, choose outcome criteria, comparison conditions and a suitable analysis explicitly. Logged chronology alone cannot establish causality or transfer from human-team research.


Operator request receipts
Private message text is excluded. Requests, boundary receipts and context-assembly records are distinct.

AN ADDITIONAL TOOL, NOT THE CONFIGURATOR
Complexity-science process observations
Complexity science and complex adaptive systems inform this tool. Gorman et al. (2017) is one team-focused reference, not its sole foundation.
Local variation, global continuity, disturbance response and informational coupling can be inspected alongside members, artifacts and coordination. The tool does not rank your team or choose a better topology.
Attractor, synchronization and fractal dimensions remain explicitly not estimated: short, uneven workflow waves do not justify those inferences.
Enable/disable or select dimensions in observations in team.yaml. No paid judge, model call or automatic organizational change.






RESEARCH NOTE / 10 NOVEMBER 2026 · GORAN MITEVSKI
Human-Team Research in Teamknitt
Human-team research guides configuration; dynamical systems theory offers a lens for following the work and informing intervention. This page maps those ideas to Teamknitt’s records and controls, without assuming that human-team findings transfer to LLM-agent teams.
Non-system and System are explanatory orientations, separate from compositional/compilational emergence and from processes, states and outcomes. IPO and IMOI connect inputs, behavioral and state mediators, outcomes and feedback. What teams do, how teams are, and what they achieve require different evidence.
Detailed research note: docs/TEAM_RESEARCH.md. The interface is a concise overview; the note contains the extended discussion and references. Dynamical analyses remain research proposals, not implemented estimators.




Selected references
These references support the interface overview; the full research note contains the extended discussion and bibliography. Original scholarly titles are retained. Complexity science is wider than any one team account; Santa Fe is not an endorsement.
Representative sources, not a claim that every theory is measured. Books/PDFs, extracts, benchmark tasks and local Foundations notes are not distributed.


STANDALONE PRODUCT / PROVENANCE
Based on Labtune
Teamknitt is a self-contained product and execution engine based on my Labtune work. Teamknitt follows my thesis research on distributed teams and their creative processes. It ships its own runtime, provider adapters, schemas and resources under the teamknitt namespace; it does not require the Labtune package, CLI or a parent runtime.
