TEAM WORKSPACE / MULTIPLE TEAMS

Teamknitt is a tool for configuring AI agent team/s, where we can set the task, follow the work in different modes, and intervene.

Configure distinct teams for one task. Inter-team communication is separate from each team’s internal topology and sharing.

i

Teams and shared task

Every team has its own configuration, provider/model settings, local limits and designated export member. The total cap applies to all teams together; adding teams does not increase it.

Team configuration

Preview member count, routing and sharing. Apply only through the displayed CLI command.

Style and external references
External URLs are references only: no skills, prompts, templates, configuration or runtimes are imported. Attach only explicitly user-authored local instructions with --style-file and --user-authored. A reference label does not establish that its source protocol is implemented.


Inter-team communication
Only configured directed links carry designated-member output from completed prior waves.

Interventions
Operate on an explicitly selected team. The browser does not queue or execute commands.
Preview first; only add --apply after review. Current-wave work may finish. Pause retains local and shared deadlines, locks and counters; stop is terminal. Connected teams synchronize at configured wave boundaries, so a paused team can hold their next boundary. Disconnected teams proceed independently within the same shared budget.
